# Bazar-bio
**Bazar-Bio** is an online platform based in Yaoundé, Cameroon, dedicated to pure, non-toxic living. We connect health-conscious consumers directly to authentic, chemical-free local food and artisanal crafts.
---

### Core Pillars

* 🌿 **100% Bio & Seasonal Food:** Zero synthetic fertilizers, artificial preservatives, or chemical pesticides. Our produce follows nature’s true calendar—available strictly in season.
* 💍 **Handcrafted Local Jewelry:** Authentic accessories made by Cameroonian artisans using locally sourced materials.
* 📍 **Digital-First to Physical:** Launching with online ordering and home delivery across Yaoundé, laying the foundation for a future physical bio-concept market.

---

### Tech & Platform
* **Focus:** Fast, mobile-first ordering experience optimized for local logistics and direct customer communication.
